# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Veriq primarily serves established local and regional service businesses, generally small to mid-sized, that want to attract more customers, improve conversion, and strengthen their digital presence over time. Typical clients include auto shops, contractors, professional services firms, gyms, and similar companies that have outgrown a weak or ineffective website.

The primary buyer wants a credible, discoverable website that gives customers a clear path to take action without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Product Purpose

Veriq helps service businesses attract customers, strengthen credibility, improve conversion, and build a durable foundation for growth through high-quality websites, local search visibility, conversion-focused functionality, and ongoing website support.

Success means creating more opportunities for the client and making the business easier to find, trust, and do business with.

## Positioning

Veriq combines polished, conversion-oriented websites with local SEO, focused customer-facing functionality, and long-term support after launch. The work begins with understanding the business and its customers rather than prescribing a template or treating visual appeal as the final outcome.

Veriq is founder-led, attentive, practical, trustworthy, and invested in each client's growth.

## Operating Context

Clients may need a new or improved marketing website, stronger local search visibility, clearer customer acquisition paths, quote or estimate tools, calculators, booking, scheduling, customer ordering, ecommerce, lead capture, customer-facing portals, or website integrations. Engagements may continue after launch through hosting, maintenance, analytics, SEO, feature development, conversion improvements, and ongoing optimization.

Clients work directly with the person designing and building the project. Veriq intentionally limits concurrent projects to provide focused execution and communication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive marketing websites and multi-page business sites
- Quote and estimate tools, calculators, booking, scheduling, ordering, ecommerce, and lead capture
- Customer-facing portals and website integrations that support a clear customer action
- Lead capture and conversion flows
- Local SEO, technical SEO, on-page optimization, and Google Business Profile optimization
- Hosting, maintenance, analytics, performance improvements, and continued website development
- Work may begin from scratch or improve an existing website
- Most business websites are currently described as taking two to six weeks; custom website functionality timelines depend on scope
- Recommendations and implementations must be grounded in the client's goals and customer journey
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, strong performance, and clear conversion paths
- Avoid exaggerated claims, fabricated metrics, and unsupported business outcomes

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Veriq name and the current overall brand direction. Veriq should feel polished, modern, premium, restrained, founder-led, attentive, practical, and trustworthy rather than like a generic agency template.

Preserve the established dark visual direction where applicable. Favor clarity and useful substance over novelty. Avoid excessive animation, visual clutter, vague AI-style copy, and designs that prioritize spectacle over comprehension or conversion.

The existing Veriq mark is available at `public/icon.svg`.

## Product Principles

1. Understand the business before choosing the solution.
2. Connect website quality, local visibility, conversion, and continued improvement.
3. Build clear, credible customer-facing experiences that make the business easier to trust and easier to work with.
4. Favor practical, durable value over hype, novelty, or unnecessary complexity.
5. Treat launch as the start of continued support and improvement when the client needs it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain accessible interactions, clear information hierarchy, keyboard usability, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive behavior across supported devices.
