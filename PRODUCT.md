## Platform

web

## Users

Veriq primarily serves established local and regional service businesses, generally small to mid-sized, that want to grow, attract more customers, and improve how they operate. Typical clients include auto shops, contractors, professional services firms, gyms, and similar companies that have outgrown a weak website or inefficient business processes.

The primary buyer wants a credible digital presence and practical systems that support the business rather than adding complexity.

## Product Purpose

Veriq helps service businesses attract customers, strengthen credibility, improve operations, and build a durable foundation for growth through high-quality websites, custom software, and ongoing digital support.

Success means creating more opportunities for the client, making the business easier to trust and do business with, and improving the workflows that affect day-to-day operations.

## Positioning

Veriq combines polished, conversion-oriented websites with software built around how a client actually operates and long-term support after launch. The work begins with understanding the business rather than prescribing a template or treating visual appeal as the final outcome.

Veriq is founder-led, attentive, practical, trustworthy, and invested in each client's growth.

## Operating Context

Clients may need a new or improved marketing website, customer acquisition paths, customer portals, quoting systems, dashboards, booking tools, internal business software, process automation, or integrations with existing tools. Engagements may continue after launch through hosting, maintenance, analytics, SEO, feature development, and ongoing optimization.

Clients work directly with the person designing and building the project. Veriq intentionally limits concurrent projects to provide focused execution and communication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive marketing websites and multi-page business sites
- Custom portals, quote systems, dashboards, booking tools, and internal software
- Lead capture and conversion flows
- Integrations and business-process automation
- Hosting, maintenance, analytics, SEO, performance improvements, and continued development
- Work may begin from scratch or improve an existing website or software system
- Most business websites are currently described as taking two to six weeks; software timelines depend on scope
- Recommendations and implementations must be grounded in how the client's business operates
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, strong performance, and clear conversion paths
- Avoid exaggerated claims, fabricated metrics, and unsupported business outcomes

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Veriq name and the current overall brand direction. Veriq should feel polished, modern, premium, restrained, founder-led, attentive, practical, and trustworthy rather than like a generic agency template.

Preserve the established dark visual direction where applicable. Favor clarity and useful substance over novelty. Avoid excessive animation, visual clutter, vague AI-style copy, and designs that prioritize spectacle over comprehension or conversion.

The existing Veriq mark is available at `public/icon.svg`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Two self-directed demonstration projects are documented in `data/projects.ts`: Iron Palace and ABC Auto Repair.
- Project images are available under `public/work/`.
- The demonstrations show design and frontend capabilities but are not client case studies and must not be presented as paid client outcomes.
- The repository contains no confirmed testimonials, client logos, performance metrics, awards, press coverage, or verified client outcome data. Future work must not fabricate them.
- Current service descriptions, FAQs, site identity, contact details, and location are maintained under `data/` and `config/`.

## Product Principles

1. Understand the business before choosing the solution.
2. Connect customer acquisition and operational improvement instead of treating them as separate concerns.
3. Build clear, credible systems that make the business easier to trust and easier to work with.
4. Favor practical, durable value over hype, novelty, or unnecessary complexity.
5. Treat launch as the start of continued support and improvement when the client needs it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain accessible interactions, clear information hierarchy, keyboard usability, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive behavior across supported devices.
